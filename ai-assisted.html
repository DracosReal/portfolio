Everything in this section was built with AI assistance. I designed each tool and system and made the decisions that shape it. My design and writing work elsewhere in this portfolio is kept separate from it on purpose.

Tools and systems

  • Resonance: A Persistent Character Architecture

    Four characters with encrypted memory, emotional state, a sense of time, and the structural ability to refuse. Designed so language models stop drifting toward generic assistant behavior and stop resetting between sessions.

    System and character design, about a year of development

  • Revision Reasoning Tool

    A judgment layer between AI document analysis and synthesis. Every AI insight needs a human decision before any merge can run, and the record proves who decided what.

    Systems design, ongoing

  • The Curious Machine

    Games made for my father as he experiences age-related cognitive decline, hosted by a dry, sardonic Machine. Six versions, including a pivot from cognitive puzzles to genuine play after watching it in use.
