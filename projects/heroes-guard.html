Heroes Guard: The Journal

Lead writer on a strategic, replayable mobile gamebook for UnderByte Studio: 20+ branching narratives with dice mechanics, creature concept and design, and a pick-up-and-play structure that lets players dip into a rich world on their own terms.

Role
Lead writer, narrative designer, creature concept designer
Studio
UnderByte Studio
Team
Primary writer with senior credit; a second writer joined later
Timeline
March 2016 to July 2017
Tools
Mindjet MindManager

The brief

The client wanted a gamebook, something between a strategy game and interactive fiction, that players could pick up for a short session and put down without losing the thread.

The challenge was building 20+ self-contained branching narratives that each felt complete on their own while belonging to a coherent world. Every story needed meaningful player choices with genuine consequence, dice mechanics that felt fair rather than arbitrary, and prose rich enough to reward readers who came for the writing as much as the game.

How each story was built

  1. Map the whole tree first. Each narrative was architected as a branching tree before a word of prose was written, mapping choice points, success and failure states, and consequence chains in Mindjet MindManager.
  2. Design the antagonists alongside it. Creature concepts were developed with the narratives, grounding the world in specific, memorable antagonists rather than generic fantasy enemies.
  3. Write to the gamebook register. Prose had to be vivid, immediate, and economical. Every word visible on a small mobile screen had to earn its place.
  4. Edit against the mechanics. Stories were edited for length, pacing, and mechanical accuracy before submission.

The architecture

Every path was documented with its success and failure states, mechanical triggers, and consequence chains. The mind map shows one complete narrative from the Black Market storyline, with the full branching structure from the start through every possible outcome across the Intelligence, Charisma, Dexterity, and Strength paths.

The format

Heroes Guard: The Journal is a strategic, replayable gamebook, a format that sits between interactive fiction and a tabletop RPG. Players read prose, make choices, and roll dice to determine outcomes across skill checks tied to Intelligence, Charisma, Dexterity, and Strength.

The gamebook structure meant every narrative had to work as a standalone piece while contributing to a larger world. Some of these branching stories later became linear fiction in my anthology, Umpkin the Bumpkin & Associated Tales.

What was delivered

  • 20+ complete branching narratives written and delivered over about a year and a half
  • A full decision tree for every narrative, mapping success and failure states, mechanical triggers, and consequence chains
  • Creature concepts and designs for the world's antagonists
  • Senior writer credit as primary writer; a second writer joined during the engagement
